Reports calls from a TornadoVM kernel (a method annotated with @Parallel or @Reduce, or taking a KernelContext or TileContext parameter) to a helper method that TornadoInsight could not analyse: the call does not resolve, or the helper is native, has no source, or lives outside the project's sources.

The other kernel inspections follow helper calls and check their bodies as if they were inlined into the kernel. For the reported call sites they could not, so problems inside those helpers are not reported.

How to fix: make sure the call resolves to a method with source in the project, or check the helper by hand before running the kernel.
